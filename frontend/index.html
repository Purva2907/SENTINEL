The Problem

Subtle document manipulation can be difficult to identify through appearance alone.

Manual Review is Subjective

Human inspection is slow and prone to missing sophisticated, pixel-level alterations.

Visual Manipulation

Advanced editing tools create forgeries that look perfect to the naked eye.

Low-Quality Documents

Poor image quality (blur, compression) does not automatically mean fraud. Signal disambiguation is required.

Forensic Signals

SENTINEL analyzes multiple orthogonal signals to build a comprehensive risk profile.

OCR Analysis

Extract and inspect document text for inconsistencies and manipulation.

QR Analysis

Detect and decode available QR regions for structural consistency.

Image Quality

Assess blur, resolution, brightness, and contrast to contextualize findings.

Typography

Inspect text consistency, alignment, and visual rendering artifacts.

Layout Analysis

Analyze structural alignment and positional consistency across the document.

Image Forensics

Look for visual manipulation indicators like compression mismatches and clone stamps.
