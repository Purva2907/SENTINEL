Human Review is Subjective & Slow

Manual examiners process documents in seconds, routinely missing microscopic font distortions, sub-pixel cloning, and mismatched compression artifacts.

Generative AI & Look-Alike Templates

Modern editing suites and diffusion models easily generate synthetic identity cards that match visual layouts while hiding altered identities.

Detached QR Code Payloads

Fraudulent documents frequently embed arbitrary QR codes or old genuine QR codes with altered printed text, creating invisible cross-signal contradictions.
